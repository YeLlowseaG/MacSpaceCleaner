Privacy

Privacy policy

Mac Space Cleaner follows a local-first approach. Scan results, file paths and cleanup history are not uploaded to the developer's servers.

On this pageData collectionFile accessNetwork and third partiesWebsite logsUpdates

Data collection

The app does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, device identifiers, usage behavior, scan results or cleanup history. It contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

File access

The app reads names, sizes and modification dates in known cache directories on your Mac so it can show potentially cleanable space. It deletes a cache directory only after you explicitly select it and confirm the cleanup.

Downloads, iCloud Drive, WeChat chat data and WPS backups are shown for storage awareness only and are not included in one-click cleanup.

macOS may separately request access to Downloads, iCloud Drive or another protected folder. You may decline; that folder will be unavailable or omitted from its storage total, while other accessible caches continue to work. Full Disk Access is not required.

Network and third-party services

The app does not send scan data to the network and does not use third-party analytics, advertising or cloud storage services. After a cache is removed, the related app may connect to its own service and download resources again; that behavior is controlled by the respective app.

Website access logs

This website is made of static pages and does not set analytics cookies. Once deployed, the hosting provider may record basic access logs for security and operations, such as IP address, time and request path. See the policy of the hosting provider you choose for details.

Policy updates

This page will be updated if the app's data handling changes. Effective date for the current version: September 20, 2026.
